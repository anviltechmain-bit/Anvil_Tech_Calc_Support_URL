  2. Optional backup uses your own Google Drive appDataFolder with the limited app-data permission only.
  3. shopkeeper app creates or updates one private JSON backup file and does not request full Google Drive access. Your account email is saved locally so future backups use the account you selected.
  4. The developer does not receive, store, or view your entries or Google Drive backup.
  5. Auto backup runs around 2:00 AM local time after Google Drive backup is connected. Android may delay work due to battery or network conditions.
  6. Notifications are used to tell you when auto sync completes or needs attention.
  7. Google AdMob may process limited device, advertising ID, diagnostic, and ad delivery data when an interstitial ad request is made.
  8. One muted interstitial ad may appear when you open History, at most once every 7 days. Sales and notes are never included in ad requests.
  9. shopkeeper app does not use website cookies inside the app. This static support page does not set cookies or run analytics scripts. Your one-time app consent choice is saved locally on your device.
  10. The app does not ask for local storage file permissions because backup uses Google Drive appDataFolder.
  11. You are responsible for protecting access to your device, Google account, and backups.

Data + Permissions
