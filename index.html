SHOP KEEPER APP

An offline sales notebook and live calculator for shopkeepers. Record sales with automatic dates and times, review your history, and count matching product sales.

Optional Google Drive backup saves your sales privately in your own account. Connect once for daily backups around 2:00 AM, or use Back Up Now and Restore. The developer does not receive your sales or backups.
